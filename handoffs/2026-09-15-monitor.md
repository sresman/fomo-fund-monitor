### 1. The digest was unreadable (`052588b`)

Rendered 168 captured items down to **9**.

- **google_news dropped from the digest entirely** -- 134 of a 168-item corpus
  (~80%), and re-queryable on demand.
- **youtube_medium must clear a 20-minute duration floor.** Sensitivity over the
  34 captured youtube_medium items, with real durations from `videos.list`:
  15m keeps 10, **20m keeps 9**, 30m keeps 4. Flat between 15 and 20 (one item
  sits in the band); steep at 30.
- **Unknown duration is KEPT**, enforced at three layers: the ISO-8601 parser
  returns `None` (never 0), a failing `videos.list` batch is logged and skipped
  rather than raised, and `digest_policy.should_queue` returns True on `None`.

Filtering happens at **enqueue**, not render. Events are still dispatched and
still committed to the dedupe bucket -- only the digest ROW is skipped -- so
re-enabling a type stays a one-line change with no backlog flood.

**Duration over title framing, and framing NOT added on top.** Measured against
the same corpus, framing keywords would have kept exactly one of 34 items, and
that one is a false positive (`Marktgeflüster`, a German market podcast
*discussing* the subject), while dropping a genuine "<name> on the AI bubble"
upload that carries no framing verb.

New file `digest_policy.py` holds the whole cut. `DigestEntry` gained
`duration_seconds`; `_DIGEST_FIELDS` is split REQUIRED/OPTIONAL so rows written
before the field existed still LOAD -- treating it as required would have
discarded the live queue on first deploy.

### 3. The surname-before-channel ordering bug (`8f94916`)

Found while answering (2). **`_classify` tested `surname_in_title` FIRST and
returned None if it failed, so `known_channels` was never reached.** An
allowlisted publisher's own upload was discarded whenever the publisher did not
put the guest's surname in the title -- common, because a show titles an episode
by its topic. The a16z episode above never entered the `youtube` dedupe bucket
at all.

**Back-catalogue sweep of all 17 allowlisted channels** (34 `search.list` calls,
~3,400 quota units): 114 candidates, 43 already HIGH on title, **71 EXCLUDEd by
the bug**, 10 of those already deduped via the manifest → **61 would newly
alert**.

| rule | newly alerts HIGH |
|---|---|
| channel membership alone | 61 |
| + 20-minute duration floor | 25 |
| **+ `is_first_party_appearance` on the description** | **4** |

A duration floor does not rescue it -- the noise on these channels is long-form
too (95m Bloomberg market shows, 96-110m All-In panels that merely mention the
name, Dwarkesh/ILTB episodes with other guests). Duration separates clips from
shows; it cannot separate "he is on it" from "they talked about him".

**Shipped:** on an allowlisted channel, qualify on surname-in-title OR
`is_first_party_appearance(title, description)` -- the gate already audited for
`podcast_rss`. Off-allowlist unchanged (surname in title → MEDIUM). Validated
against the real sweep: **4 newly promoted, 0 regressions** among the 43.
2 of the 4 are genuine; 2 are the known mention-vs-appearance false positive.

Classification now runs **after** `videos.list`, because `search.list` truncates
descriptions to ~120 chars. `check_youtube` is two passes: collect candidates →
one batched `videos.list` (`contentDetails,snippet`) → classify on the full
description. No extra API cost -- that call already existed for durations.

### THE FINDING: the corpus lost nothing, and that was luck

**The ordering bug has cost zero real appearances to date.**

- **a16z 2026-08-31** -- the only genuine appearance it dropped post-seed
  (2026-08-09+). The a16z podcast FEED caught it and it alerted. Net loss: zero.
- **All-In 2025-08-09** -- genuine, but pre-seed, so it would have been seeded
  rather than alerted regardless.

**The RSS feeds have been covering for the bug.** That is not design.
**Seven of the 17 allowlisted channels have NO feed configured:**

```
Dwarkesh Patel · CNBC Television · Bloomberg Television
iConnections · Sohn Conference Foundation · Heller House
```

(Seven at the time of the sweep; Limitless Podcast was then removed from the
allowlist for unrelated reasons, leaving six.)

An appearance on any of those whose title omits the surname vanished with no
trace at all -- not an alert, not the digest, not the dedupe bucket. Zero such
cases in the back catalogue so far. That is the structural hole the fix closes,
and the reason it was worth closing before it cost anything.

### Known issues

- **The allowlist assumes every entry is a VENUE, not a commentator.** On an
  allowlisted channel the **surname-in-title path bypasses the description
  gate** -- deliberately, since a venue naming him in the title is the strongest
  signal available. The cost is that a commentator on the list would alert on
  every "<surname> says X" headline it ever published, and no description gate
  can stop it. That assumption holds for the remaining 16 entries. It is the
  reason **Limitless Podcast was removed** (see Resolved, above); re-check it
  before adding anything to `known_channels`.
- **Mention vs appearance still unsolved.** The 2 false positives among the 4
  newly promoted (Bloomberg `Big Take`, TBPN `Martin Shkreli Breaks Down the
  Collapse`) are both this. A framing stem whose subject is someone else still
  passes.
- **The duration filter is unfalsified, not validated.** None of the 9 digest
  survivors is a genuine first-party appearance -- the corpus contained no
  unallowlisted one to test against. It demonstrably removes noise; it has not
  been shown to preserve a real recovery case. Likewise keep-on-missing: zero of
  34 items had an unresolved duration, so that path is unit-tested only.
- Carried over: **repo is still public**; `situational-awareness.com` is
  unmonitorable; the Aleph compilation inconsistency (SD-A56 addressed the
  2026-08-12 one).
